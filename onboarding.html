Set up in three steps

1

Open the Mova Flow app on this computer

The extension records; the Mova Flow desktop app transcribes — on this computer, or on the host it's connected to (your PC with a GPU, say) — and keeps every recording in its History. The extension doesn't work without it: install it once, set the host up there, and keep it running (it lives in the menu bar / tray). Version 0.4.12 or newer.

2

Press Record in the call

A record button appears right in the call's own controls — next to mic and camera in Google Meet, in Zoom's bottom bar (join from the browser), first in Teams' meeting controls, in Jitsi's toolbox. Press it again to stop; the transcript comes back in the extension and lands in the app's History.

3

The first time on a call, one more key

Chrome only lets an extension record a tab once you've called on it there — a button inside the page doesn't count. So the first time you press Record on a call, the icon lights up with REC; then press ⌥⇧R, or right-click the call → Record this call with Mova Flow. Recording starts by itself, and holds for the rest of the call.

The side panel

Press ⧉ in the popup and the whole extension moves into the browser's side panel — it stays open during the call instead of closing on the first click elsewhere, and the recording keeps going while you switch tabs.

  • Record — start, stop, and the finished transcript.
  • History — the Mova Flow app's History, searchable; copy, download, delete, or transcribe a recording that's still waiting.
  • Upload — any audio or video file, transcribed by the app.
  • Settings — whether the app is running and where it transcribes.

Settings can make the toolbar icon open the side panel instead of the popup. A click that opens the side panel doesn't count as calling on the extension, though — use the key or the right-click for the first recording on a call.

Your microphone stays yours

Your side of the conversation is taken from the audio the call itself sends to the others — so it's silent whenever you're muted, and this extension never asks for or opens your microphone. (Zoom sends no such audio, so there it's the microphone Zoom itself opened, silenced while Zoom shows you muted.) On Meet, names come from its live captions, which are switched on only on your screen while you record.

Once it says “Connected to Mova Flow”, you're all set — close this tab and join a call. Privacy policy
