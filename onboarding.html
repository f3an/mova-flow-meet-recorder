MOVA FLOW — SETUP

Meeting notes, without the cloud

Mova Flow records your Google Meet calls and transcribes them on your own Mova Flow host — nothing is ever sent anywhere else. Three quick things to set up, then you're recording.

1

Look for the button in Meet

Once this is set up, a record button appears right in Meet's own call controls — no need to find the extension icon mid-call.

The green button on the right is Mova Flow.
2

Connect to your Mova Flow host

The same host your desktop app uses — find it automatically if it's on this network, or enter it by hand.

3

Allow microphone access

Meet never plays your own voice back through the tab — only the other participants'. Without this, transcripts would be missing everything you said. Chrome can only ask for this on a visible page like this one, not from the hidden recorder that runs during a call.

You're all set. Close this tab and start a Meet call.
