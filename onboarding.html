Set up in two steps

1

Connect to your Mova Flow host

The same host your desktop app uses. Find it automatically if it's on this network, or copy the address and the access secret key from the host's Server tab.

2

Press Record in Meet

A record button appears right in Meet's own call controls — no need to find the extension icon mid-call. Press it again to stop; the transcript comes back in the extension's popup.

This one is Mova Flow ↑

Your microphone stays yours

Your side of the conversation is taken from the audio Meet itself sends to the others — so it's silent whenever you're muted, and this extension never asks for or opens your microphone. Names come from Meet's live captions, which are switched on only on your screen while you record.

Once the host is saved, you're all set — close this tab and join a call. Privacy policy
